How the radar reads the snapshot

  1. Pre-token first. Only protocols that record no token (no CoinGecko id, no symbol) can rank. Protocols whose token already trades are the comparables, never the board.
  2. Comparable multiples. Each category is priced by its own traded tokens — median market-cap/TVL — so a Risk Curator is not priced like a DEX. Thin categories fall back to the global median.
  3. Early, not just big. The board starts at the $50M watchlist floor but reaches below it: 158 of 198 rows sit under the floor. Growth, chain breadth and time running without a token move a protocol up or down.
  4. Farm plans, not slogans. Every row carries five steps with amounts, chains and a time horizon, generated from the protocol's own category and chains.
